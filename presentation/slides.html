01 / Challenge

Problem — Who, What, and Why It Hurts

SupplyShield AI
👤 The Target Audience

Global supply chain directors, port operations commanders, pharmaceutical logistics managers, and multi-modal fleet coordinators managing high-value, time-critical international cargo.

⚠️ The Core Problem

Global transit routes face unpredictable disruptions—port strikes, canal blockages, extreme snowstorms. Data is siloed in legacy ERPs, radar feeds, and email chains with zero automated correlation.

💥 Quantified Pain

$10B+ annual freight delay penalties.

4.5 Hours MTTR: Average time lost manually cross-referencing spreadsheets.

Irreversible Spoilage: High-value biological medicines ruined during transit delays.

02 / Solution

Solution — What We Built & How It Works

SupplyShield AI
🛡️ Intelligent Command Center
  • Aggregated Disruption Hub: Continuous ingestion of port closures, weather alerts, and strikes mapped against global transit corridors.
  • Dynamic Risk Engine: Continuous scoring (0–100) weighting priority, disruption proximity, delivery SLA deadlines, and IoT thermal telemetry.
  • Automated Recovery Workspace: Real-time alternative route generation, cost/time trade-off simulation, and carrier assignment.
⚡ The AI Copilot Differentiator
  • Real-time Data Querying: Query active disruptions and critical shipments through natural language.
  • Context-Aware Interventions: Evaluates fleet availability (including refrigerated vehicles) to dispatch mitigation orders in seconds.
  • Explainable Risk Factors: Transparent breakdown of each risk rating rather than a black-box percentage.
